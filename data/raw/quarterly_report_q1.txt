Quarterly Financial Summary — Q1 2026
Prepared for: Sample User (Personal Finance Review)
Period Covered: January 1, 2026 – March 31, 2026

Overview

This quarter saw overall income remain stable while discretionary spending
increased slightly compared to the previous quarter. Total income across
the three months was £13,600.00, made up primarily of salary payments
totalling £12,000.00 and £1,600.00 in miscellaneous income including a
small freelance project and a tax rebate received in February.

Spending by Category

Housing costs, including rent and utilities, accounted for the largest
share of spending at £4,650.00 for the quarter. Groceries and household
essentials came to £1,240.60. Transportation costs, including fuel and
two train season tickets, totalled £780.25. Dining out and entertainment
spending was £560.10 across the quarter, with February being the highest
month due to a birthday celebration.

A notable one-off expense occurred in March: a replacement laptop was
purchased for £1,150.00, reference PUR-MAR-0308, to replace an ageing
work device. This was treated as a planned expense rather than an
emergency purchase, having been budgeted for since December.

Savings Activity

£2,400.00 was transferred into the linked savings account during the
quarter, split across two transfers in January and March. The savings
account balance at the end of the quarter stood at £9,850.00, up from
£7,450.00 at the start of the year.

Outlook

Based on current spending patterns, projected income for Q2 2026 is
expected to remain similar at approximately £13,500.00, with housing
costs likely to rise slightly following an announced increase in the
monthly service charge from April.

End of Report.
